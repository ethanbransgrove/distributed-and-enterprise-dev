## Organisational Responsibilites
- **Producers:** maintain accurate product information, handle stock and seasonal availability as well as suppling relevant products and certification information.
- **Customers:** Browse products, place orders and provide payment information through the correct channels for payment processing.
- **Marketplace Administration:** Coordinate marketplace operations and manage producer onboarding. 
- **Finance:** Oversee payment records, commission calculations, help producer settlements and associated audit trails for tax purposes.
- **Quality and Compliance:** Support food safety processes, have relevant tracebility records in the event of a food safety concern, and handle product claims about organic status and other claims.
- **External Parties:** Provide the integrations required for payment processing and organic certification verification.

## Strategic alignment
### business objectives
- supporting local producers: The system gives local producers a way to list products, manage stock, and update seasonal availability. Helping local producers get to a wider array of customers
- Improving customer access: A centralised system helps customers to discover local produce easier
- Supporting seasonal produce: Accurate information on avalability helps customers understand which products can be purchased at certain times in a year.
- Builds trust: Displaying information on the farm including origin, harvest dates, allergen information, and organic certification status helps build trust with the customers and what they are buying 

### operational reliability and compliance
- data accuracy: validation prevents incomplete or invalid product information
- availability accuracy: automatic stock updates reduce the risk of customers ordering no longer available products
- certification integrity: verification of organic certification must be shown to show clarity to customers
- food safety information: clear allergen and bes-before information supports informed pruchasing.

# Business Process Analysis

## Major Business Processes
1. Producer onboarding and certification verification
2. product listing and availability management
3. customer registration
4. product discovery
5. order placement
6. payment processing
7. order fulfilment
8. producer settlment
9. quality and food safety incident management
10. surplus produce
11. sustainability reporting
12. education and community content

## Business Process Analysis
| Business process | Purpose and key activities | Main responsibility | Relationship with other processes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Producer onboarding and certification verification** | Register producers and so they can sell. Verify relevant certification claims | Marketplace admin and producers provide information. | Enables product listing which means customers can buy. |
| **Product listing and availability management** | Submit product details, validate information, publish listings, and maintains stock and seasonal availability | Producers and marketplace platform | Supports product discovery and determines which products can be ordered. |
| **Customer registration** | Capture the information needed for customer accounts | Customers and marketplace platform | May support ordering and customer communication |
| **Product discovery** | xAllow customers to browse and search for local products and view correct product information | Customers and marketplace platform | Depends on the published listings and accuracy of availability |
| **Order placement** | Capture customer selections and create orders, could involve multiple producers | Customers and marketplace | Depends on product availability and starts the payment processes |
| **Payment processing** | Process or simulate payments and manage payment outcomes | Marketplace finance functionalities and payment service | Supports orders and contributes towards producer settlement calculations |
| **Order fulfilment** | Coordinate delivery or collection and communicate responsibilities with customers and producers | Producers and marketplace operations | Follows order placement and provides information about order completion |
| **Producer settlment** | Calculate producer payments, include the 5% commission and maintain records of each settlement | Marketplace finance functions | Depends on relevant order and payment records |
| **Quality and food safety incident management** | Maintain relevant product and traceability information and support investigation of food safety concerns. | Quality and compliance function, with producers help | Depends on product, producer and customer order records |
| **Surplus produce** | Support discounts or other methods to reduce food waste. | Producers and marketplace operations | Connects inventory information with product discovery and purchasing |
| **Sustainability reportingt** | Report relevant sustainability information, including food miles | Marketplace operations | Depends on appropriate product information and relevant records  |
| **Education and community content** | Provide educational information about local food and related community activities | Marketplace operations and relevant contributors | Supports the wider marketplace mission rather than directly controlling product publication |


## Organisational Responsibilites
- **Producers:** maintain accurate product information, handle stock and seasonal availability as well as suppling relevant products and certification information.
- **Customers:** Browse products, place orders and provide payment information through the correct channels for payment processing.
- **Marketplace Administration:** Coordinate marketplace operations and manage producer onboarding. 
- **Finance:** Oversee payment records, commission calculations, help producer settlements and associated audit trails for tax purposes.
- **Quality and Compliance:** Support food safety processes, have relevant tracebility records in the event of a food safety concern, and handle product claims about organic status and other claims.
- **External Parties:** Provide the integrations required for payment processing and organic certification verification.

## Strategic Alignment

### Business Objectives
- supporting local producers: The system gives local producers a way to list products, manage stock, and update seasonal availability. Helping local producers get to a wider array of customers
- Improving customer access: A centralised system helps customers to discover local produce easier
- Supporting seasonal produce: Accurate information on avalability helps customers understand which products can be purchased at certain times in a year.
- Builds trust: Displaying information on the farm including origin, harvest dates, allergen information, and organic certification status helps build trust with the customers and what they are buying 

### Operational Reliability and Compliance
- data accuracy: validation prevents incomplete or invalid product information
- availability accuracy: automatic stock updates reduce the risk of customers ordering no longer available products
- certification integrity: verification of organic certification must be shown to show clarity to customers
- food safety information: clear allergen and bes-before information supports informed pruchasing.

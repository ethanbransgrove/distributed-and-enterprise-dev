# Use Case 1 Sprint Plan 

Producer Product Listing and Inventory Management 

This proposed plan divides Task 1 between four people, from preparation through the final Camunda demonstration. 

## Sprint 0 Preparation 

**Start 28th September 2026 | Non-assessed preparation**

#### Rodrigo - Stakeholders and requirements 

- Read both PDFs and obtain the complete base case study from the module materials. 
- List stakeholders, responsibilities and initial goals; distinguish confirmed requirements from questions. 
- Record all product information named in Use Case 1 and ask which ask which fields are mandatory or conditional. 
- Prepare requirement notes linked to their source and initial backlog items. 

#### Ethan - Repository and publication scope 

- Create or check the shared GitHub/GitLab repository and invite all members. 
- Check that the module team can access it and add an initial README. 
- Identify the main enterprise processes from the full case study. 
- Write the proposed listing and publication scope for module-team discussion. 

#### Rory - Inventory scope and environment 

- Write the proposed inventory and seasonal availability scope. 
- List the zero-stock, replenishment and out-of-season scenarios from the use case. 
-   Check the Camunda version and tools required in your practical sessions. 
- Verify access to those tools and record any setup problems. 

#### Zuhaib - Team working and verification 

- Set up the agreed communication channel, meeting schedule and project board. 
- Add owners, priorities and evidence links to the initial backlog. 
- Investigate an external or simulated certification service and its possible outcomes. 
- Start a contribution log and short list of risks and unresolved questions. 

## Sprint 1 Strategic analysis and setup 

**Start 12th October 2026**

#### Zuhaib - Stakeholder analysis and validation rules 

- Document stakeholder goals, dependencies and concerns using the full case study. 
- Begin i* Strategic Dependency and Strategic Rationale models; review them with everyone. 
- Draft the product field list and proposed mandatory, conditional and invalid-value rules. 
- Turn listing validation requirements into backlog items with completion criteria. 

#### Rodrigo - Strategic BPMN and publication planning 

- Coordinate a group-created strategic BPMN model covering the overall enterprise. 
- Identify major processes, organisational responsibilities and their interactions.

#### Ethan - Inventory analysis and planning 

- Contribute inventory and seasonal availability responsibilities to the strategic analysis. 
- Define functionality #2’s inputs, outputs and proposed stock and season rules. 
- List normal and exceptional inventory scenarios, including invalid updates. 
- Create inventory backlog items and check that the approved scope is substantial enough. 

#### Rory - 4 External boundaries and project evidence

- Define a proposed certification-service contract for success, failure and unavailability.

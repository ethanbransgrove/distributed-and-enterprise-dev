## Main Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Goals and needs | Relevance to Use Case 1 |
|---|---|---|
| **Local food producers** | Reach more customers and list products through an intuitive system. Update stock and seasonal availability without extensive technical knowledge or training. | Submit product information, correct invalid details, and maintain quantity and availability. |
| **Young professionals and families** | Buy locally sourced food despite limited time and difficulty visiting multiple farm shops during opening hours. | Depend on clear product information and accurate availability when deciding what to buy. |
| **Community groups** — educational institutions, care facilities and voluntary organisations | Find reliable and consistent local suppliers for catering. | Use product listings and availability information to assess whether suppliers can meet their needs. |
| **Independent restaurants and cafés** | Source ingredients locally while reducing the administrative effort of managing multiple small suppliers. | Benefit from consistent product information and current stock levels when assessing available ingredients. |
| **Bristol Regional Food Network** | Connect local producers and consumers, support traceability and sustainability, and reduce food waste. | Has an organisational interest in accurate listings, clear product claims and dependable availability. A specific administrator role for this use case is not defined. |

**Sources:** Case Study - v2.pdf, pages 1–2; Task 1 - Use Cases.pdf, page 1.

**Note:** Stakeholder goals are drawn from the case study. Their relevance to Use Case 1 is our analysis of those requirements.
